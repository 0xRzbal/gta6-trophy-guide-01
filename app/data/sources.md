# Sources

Every trophy row in this guide cites one or more of the community sources below by number.
The links open only when you follow them; the app itself fetches nothing from the network.

1. PSNProfiles (GTA VI trophy list & guides) - https://www.psnprofiles.com/trophies/gta6
2. Exophase (Grand Theft Auto VI trophies) - https://www.exophase.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi/
3. PlayStationTrophies.org (trophy guide forums) - https://www.playstationtrophies.org/
4. GTA VI Wiki (Fandom [mission & collectible details) - https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/
5. GameFAQs (GTA VI walkthrough & FAQ) - https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/
6. YouTube (GTA VI trophy guide videos) - https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=gta+6+trophy+guide

Notes on how to read the guide: "**" marks a story stage, "collectibles" means world collectibles, and "activities" covers side play. A "missable" badge means the trophy can be permanently missed; clear those before finishing the story.
