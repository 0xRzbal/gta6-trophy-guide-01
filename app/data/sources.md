# Sources

This guide was written from the sources below. Every trophy's entry names the sources used for it. The app shows these as plain text and never fetches them; the links simply open when you follow them.

- **Exophase - GTA VI trophy list** — the official 55-trophy PlayStation 5 list, names, descriptions and grades as PlayStation publishes them. https://www.exophase.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi-ps5/trophies/
- **PSNProfiles - GTA VI trophy guide** — community roadmap, misable flags and step-by-step tips for the trophy set. https://psnprofiles.com/trophies/20000-grand-theft-auto-vi
- **Official PSN description** — the in-game description text as PlayStation publishes it for every trophy. https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/grand-theft-auto-vi/
- **Kaeru collectible & exploration guide** — locations for collectible cards, WAiNK signs, vehicles, meditation spots and exploration. https://kaeru.gg/guides/gta6-collectibles
