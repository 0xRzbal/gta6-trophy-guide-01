# Sources

Every trophy guide in this tracker cites one or more of the sources below. The official
PlayStation list is the source of every trophy's name, description and grade; the other
sources back up the where/how steps. Links are shown for you to follow; the app fetches
nothing at run time.

- **official** — PlayStation official trophy list for Grand Theft Auto VI (frozen 2026-11-18), mirrored on Exophase. https://www.exophase.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi-playstation-5/trophies/
- **powerpyx** — PowerPyx GTA VI trophy and 100% guide, with activity and collectible maps. https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-trophy-guide/
- **psnprofiles** — PSNProfiles community tracking and route notes for GTA VI. https://psnprofiles.com/trophies/14436-grand-theft-auto-vi
- **gtavideos** — GTA Series Videos (YouTube) mission walkthroughs and collectible runs. https://www.youtube.com/@GTASeriesVideos
- **reddit** — r/GTA6 community megathread for secrets, random events and online trophies. https://www.reddit.com/r/GTA6/
- **community** — Community companion guides (fandom wiki mirrors) for activities and minigames. https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto_VI
- **missable** — Missable-trophy notes compiled from the sources above (stranger strands, partner events, random encounters).

The official names and descriptions are reproduced as PlayStation publishes them; all guide
steps are written fresh for this entry.
